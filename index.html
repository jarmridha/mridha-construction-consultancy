What kind of projects do you undertake? +

We support residential and commercial construction, interior fit-out, renovation and engineering consultancy assignments, depending on project scope and location.

Can you support a project from planning to handover? +

Yes. Services can cover planning, BOQ and estimation, coordination, site supervision, QA/QC, execution support and final handover documentation.

Yes. These services can be provided as part of a wider project scope or as focused consultancy support.
